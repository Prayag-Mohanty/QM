# About QM Prayag

**QM Prayag** is the home of quiz sets written and hosted by **Prayag Mohanty**.

For years these quizzes lived on SlideShare. This site is their new permanent
home: every set can be flipped through slide by slide right in your browser,
downloaded as the original PDF or PowerPoint, and shared with your quiz club.

## Using these quizzes

Feel free to use the questions for practice, college quiz clubs, office quizzes
or friendly quiz nights. If you host them publicly, please credit
*QM Prayag (Prayag Mohanty)* and link back to the quiz page.
