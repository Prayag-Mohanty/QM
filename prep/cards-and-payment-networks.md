## Card networks

| Network | Origin and name | Logo, slogans and trivia | Now |

| **Visa** | Began as **BankAmericard** (Bank of America, 1958). In the "**Fresno Drop**", 60,000 unsolicited cards were mailed to Fresno, California. **Dee Hock** renamed it **Visa** in 1976 because the word sounds the same in almost every language and suggests acceptance everywhere. | The **blue, white and gold** bands stood for California's blue sky, white clouds and golden hills. Long-time **Olympic** sponsor since 1986; its ads mocked Amex with "and they don't take American Express". Also a **FIFA** sponsor. The 2008 IPO was then the largest in US history. Card numbers start with **4**. | CEO **Ryan McInerney**; HQ San Francisco. A US judge approved the long-running **swipe-fee settlement** with merchants in **June 2026**. |
| **Mastercard** | **Interbank Card Association** (1966), a group of banks forming a rival to BankAmericard. The "**Priceless**" campaign (1997, McCann). It has a sonic logo. Ex-CEO **Ajay Banga** (Pune-born) is now **World Bank president**. Card numbers start with **5**, and since 2017 also **2**. | CEO **Michael Miebach**; HQ Purchase, New York. IPO in 2006. |
| **Maestro** | Mastercard's **debit** brand (1991). | Blue and red circles. Being **phased out in Europe from July 2023** and replaced by Debit Mastercard. Its sister brands are **Cirrus** (Mastercard's ATM network) and, on Visa's side, **Plus**, **Electron** (retired) and **Interlink**. |  |

The **traveller's cheque** (1891) was invented by **Marcellus Berry** after J.C. Fargo struggled to get cash in Europe. Its charge card dates from 1958. | The **Centurion** (Roman soldier) logo. The invitation-only **Centurion "Black Card"**. Slogan "**Don't leave home without it**" (actor Karl Malden). Warren Buffett bought in after the **1963 salad-oil scandal** and still holds a big stake. It's a **closed loop**: Amex both issues the cards and runs the network. Numbers start with **34 or 37**, are **15 digits** long, and have a **4-digit code on the front**. | CEO **Stephen Squeri**.\* |
| **Diners Club** | **Frank McNamara** forgot his wallet at **Major's Cabin Grill**, New York (1949). He returned on **8 Feb 1950** to pay with a **cardboard card**, the "**First Supper**". The first multipurpose **charge card**, launched with Ralph Schneider and initially accepted at 27 restaurants. | Owned by Citigroup, then **Discover (2008)**, and now **Capital One**. In India, cards are issued with **HDFC Bank**. Numbers start with **36**. | Under Capital One, which bought Discover for about $35 bn (closed **May 2025**). |
| **Discover** | Launched by **Sears** in 1985, with a debut ad in the **1986 Super Bowl**. | Pioneer of **cashback** and no annual fee. Numbers start with **6011 or 65**. | **Capital One** owns it and is moving its own debit and credit cards from Mastercard onto the Discover network (2025–26). |
| **JCB** | **Japan Credit Bureau** (1961), set up by Sanwa Bank. | Japan's only international network. |  |
| **UnionPay** | China, 2002 (state-backed). | The world's **largest by cards issued**. |  |
| **RuPay** | **NPCI**, 2012. The name joins **Rupee + Payment**. | India's domestic network. RuPay credit cards could be linked to **UPI** from 2022. Mastercard reportedly lobbied the US government against it (2018). Numbers usually start with 6. |  |
| **Mir** | Russia, 2015, after **Visa and Mastercard sanctions** over Crimea (2014). | "**Mir**" means both "**world**" and "**peace**". After 2022, Visa and Mastercard left Russia entirely. |  |
| **Others** | **Elo** (Brazil), **Interac** (Canada), **Bancontact** (Belgium), **Carte Bancaire** (France), **Troy** (Turkey), **Jaywan** (UAE). |  |  |

**The card number:** the first digit is the **Major Industry Identifier**. 3 = travel/entertainment (Amex, Diners), 4 = Visa, 5 = Mastercard, 6 = Discover/RuPay. The last digit is a check digit computed by the **Luhn algorithm** (Hans Peter Luhn, IBM).

## Meal and benefit cards, and card firsts

### Meal and benefit cards

| Brand | Story |
| --- | --- |
| **Sodexo → Pluxee** | **Pierre Bellon** founded Sodexo in **Marseille in 1966** as a ship and hospital caterer. The name comes from the French **"société d'exploitation hotelière"**.\* Its benefits arm, with **meal vouchers and cards**, was **spun off in Feb 2024 as Pluxee**, listed on Euronext Paris. In India, "Sodexo coupons" became **Pluxee** cards. Sodexo also caters at Wimbledon, Roland-Garros and the Olympic Village.\* |
| **Edenred / Ticket Restaurant** | **Ticket Restaurant** was launched in France in **1962** by **Jacques Borel**, the "inventor of the meal voucher". Edenred was **spun off from Accor in 2010**.\* |
| **Zeta** (India) | Founded by **Bhavin Turakhia** (Directi, Flock) and Ramki Gaddipati. Started with **tax-benefit meal cards**, then moved to card-issuing tech for banks. A **SoftBank** and **Mastercard**-backed unicorn.\* |
| **Why meal cards exist in India** | The long-standing rule made meals worth up to **₹50 per meal** from an employer **tax-free** (old regime). Check whether this still applies under the new tax regime.\* |

### Card and cash "firsts" (a classic timeline question)

- **1887**: Edward Bellamy's novel *Looking Backward* imagines a "**credit card**" and coins the term.
- **1928**: the **Charga-Plate**, a metal plate used by department stores.
- **1946**: **"Charg-It"**, from **John Biggins** at Flatbush National Bank, Brooklyn. The first bank-issued card.
- **1950**: **Diners Club**, the first multipurpose charge card.
- **1958**: **BankAmericard** (later Visa), and **Amex** launches its card.
- **1966**: **Barclaycard**, the **first credit card outside the US**.
- **27 Jun 1967**: the **first ATM**, at **Barclays Enfield**, London. Inventor **John Shepherd-Barron** was **born in Shillong, India**. The PIN became **4 digits** because his wife could only remember four (he wanted six). Actor **Reg Varney** made the first withdrawal.
- **1960s**: the **magnetic stripe**, by **Forrest Parry** at IBM. His wife **Dorothea** suggested using her **clothes iron** to fix the tape to the card.
- **1974**: **Roland Moreno** (France) patents the **smart card chip**, leading to **EMV** (Europay, Mastercard, Visa).
- **1997**: **Mobil Speedpass** key fob, the first mainstream **contactless** payment.
- **2014**: **Apple Pay**.

## India and Kerala

### India: cards and payments facts

- **First credit card in India**: **Central Bank of India (1980)**. Diners Club had come in earlier as a charge card.\*
- **First ATM in India**: **HSBC**, Mumbai, **1987**.\*
- **SBI Card** (1998) began as a JV with **GE Capital**, later bought out by SBI and **Carlyle**. Listed in 2020 (SBICARD).\*
- **HDFC Bank** is the largest card issuer and issues **Diners Club** cards.\*
- **The RBI's 2021 card bans**: **Mastercard** was barred from adding new customers (July 2021 to June 2022), as were **Amex** and **Diners** (2021), for breaking **data-localisation** rules. This pushed banks to issue RuPay and Visa.\*
- **RuPay** cards go with the **Jan Dhan** accounts and come with accident insurance.\*
- **NPCI products**: UPI, RuPay, IMPS (2010), **NACH**, **FASTag/NETC**, **AePS** (Aadhaar-enabled payments), **BBPS** (bill payments) and **NFS** (the ATM switch).\*
- **Kisan Credit Card** (1998), a NABARD scheme.\*

### Kerala and Thrissur hooks

- **Gulf remittances**: Kerala receives about a **fifth of India's inward remittances**, much of it through exchange houses.\*
  - **LuLu Exchange** belongs to **M.A. Yusuff Ali**, from **Nattika, Thrissur**.
  - **UAE Exchange** was founded by **B.R. Shetty** (Udupi) and collapsed in 2020 along with NMC Health.
  - **Muthoot**, **Manappuram** and Federal Bank all run money-transfer services.
- **Federal Bank** (Aluva, founded by **K.P. Hormis**): a big share of India's NRI remittances. Its co-branded cards include **Scapia**, a travel card.\*
- **Kerala Bank** (2019) merged the district co-operative banks; in Malayalam it's **Kerala Sahakarana Bank**.\*
- **KSFE** (Kerala State Financial Enterprises, **Thrissur** HQ): state-run **chitty** (chit funds).\*
- **Kochi Metro's Kochi1 card** (Axis Bank, 2017) was an early **open-loop co-branded metro card**.\*
- Thrissur is home to **South Indian Bank, CSB, Dhanlaxmi and ESAF**, all of which issue debit and RuPay cards.
