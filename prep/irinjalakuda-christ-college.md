## Christ College, Irinjalakuda

**The basics:**

- Founded in **1956** by **Fr. Gabriel Chiramel CMI**, its first principal and later a **Padma Bhushan** recipient. The first manager was Fr. Clemens CMI.
- Inaugurated on **13 Oct 1957** by Kerala Governor **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao**.
- Run by the **CMI** (Carmelites of Mary Immaculate, Devamatha Province). The CMI was founded in **1831** by **St Kuriakose Elias Chavara**.
- Motto: **"Jeevitha Prabha"** ("Light of Life").
- Started with **240 students and 14 teachers**.
- Affiliated to the **University of Calicut** (since 1968; Kerala University before that). Now **autonomous**.
- In **2022** it became the **first college in Thrissur district with NAAC A++**.
- The sister CMI institution is **Christ University, Bengaluru**, whose former chancellor Paul Achandy CMI is a Christ College alumnus.

**Sports powerhouse:** **10 consecutive Calicut University overall titles**, the latest in Aug 2026 with a record **3,413 points**. Its indoor stadium is named after Fr. Gabriel.

## Irinjalakuda: places, business and trivia

### The name

There are several theories:

- **"Iru" + "Chaal"**: two streams.
- **"Irinjalikoodal"**: the confluence of two rivers (*koodal* = merging).
- **"Iru njyaala koda"**: Mahabali's gift of two worlds to Vamana.
- **"Virinja Alin Kuda"**: the umbrella of a spreading **banyan**. The central banyan is still a landmark.

The town's nickname is **"Varadanangalude Nadu"**.

### Koodalmanikyam Temple

- Famous as one of the very few temples in India dedicated to **Bharata**, Rama's brother, worshipped here as **Sangameswara**.\*
- Inscriptions date to about **854 CE**. Some scholars think it was once a **Jain** centre.
- The **Thachudaya Kaimal** controlled it until 1971. It's now run by a trust, with the **District Collector** as chief trustee.
- Known for its **lotus garland (Thamaramala)** offering, the **brinjal nivedyam** offering for stomach ailments, and the annual **Utsavam** with 17 elephants. There is **no usual deeparadhana or incense ritual**.\*
- Legend says the name comes from a ruby (*manikyam*) that **merged** (*koodal*) into the idol's glow.\*

### Art and culture

- **Natana Kairali**: **Koodiyattam** training (G. Venu).
- **Ammannur Chachu Chakyar Smaraka Gurukulam**: Koodiyattam.
- **Unnayi Warrier Smaraka Kalanilayam**: **Kathakali**, next to the temple.
- **Walden Pond House**: a cultural space and film-shoot venue.
- **St Thomas Cathedral**: seat of the **Syro-Malabar Eparchy of Irinjalakuda**.
- **Karukulangara Narasimhaswamy Temple**.
- **Tipu Sultan**'s army camped at **Thazhekkad** (Dec 1789).
- Literacy is about **96%**.
- **St Joseph's College** is the only college in Kerala with a government-aided BSc Biotechnology.
- The **Thrissur Rural Police** HQ moved here in May 2023.
