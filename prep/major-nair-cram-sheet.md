What Major Chandrakanth Nair is likely to ask at the Christ Com finals on Nov 5, 2026, built from his two recent prelims and a scan of the past year's news.

## How Major Nair sets questions

He rarely asks a bare fact. In 31 prelims questions, the answer is almost always a **name that links two worlds**: a brand and a book, a city and an artist, a law and a TV show. Prepare by learning why things are named what they are, not lists of facts.

Major Chandrakanth Nair is a former Army medical officer turned quizmaster, based in Kerala. He gave a TEDx talk called *The Question of a Question*. His two recent prelims were Christ Com 2026 (16 questions, business) and The Monty Quiz 8.0 at NIT Jamshedpur (15 questions, general).

| Pattern | Share of his 31 questions | His examples | What to prepare |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shared names** (one name, two worlds) | about 8 | Miyazaki (a city in Kyushu and the Ghibli animator); Maestro (a conductor and a Mastercard debit card); Great Lakes (a business school and the lakes); Rose Bowl (Pasadena stadium and Hampshire's ground); TGIF (Google's all-hands and the restaurant chain); 1492 Pictures (Columbus's voyage and Chris Columbus's company); PATRIOT Act (the law and Hasan Minhaj's show); Cambridge Analytica and the university | For every brand you revise, ask what else shares its name. |
| **Name origins** | about 6 | Salazar Slytherin (named after Portugal's dictator); Smirnoff (the Smirnov family); Listerine (Joseph Lister, made a baron); Anansi; *The Two Towers* | The stories behind brand and character names. |
| **Logos, colours and trademarks** | about 4 | Allahabad Bank's logo (Triveni Sangam); ASI's logo (the Sanchi stupa); T-Mobile's magenta trademark; Mondrian (as a connect) | Logo meanings of Indian PSUs, banks and institutions. Trademarked colours: Tiffany blue, Cadbury purple, UPS brown, Louboutin red soles. |
| **Film, books and pop culture meeting the real world** | about 7 | *Shutter Island* recreating Klimt's *The Kiss*; *Joy* and the Miracle Mop; Hello Barbie's hacking scare; Tolkien; Gaiman; Chabon; Rowling | Biopics of brands and films that borrow from art (see the films doc). |
| **An old fact tied to a 2026 headline** | about 5 | "Inyenzi" (cockroach), tied to India's 2026 "Cockroach Janta Party" protest; the BRICS New Development Bank; the Thar; the "Modi kurta" at Jade Blue | Skim the year's viral moments and ask what older fact each one connects to. |
| **India and Kerala business** | about 6 | Manappuram Finance (1949, Valapad, Thrissur; V.P. Nandakumar); Allahabad Bank; Jade Blue; Mahindra Thar; IPC; Great Lakes | Thrissur's businesses (see the Kerala section below). |
| **Sport and sponsorship** | about 2 | Victoria Bitter on Australia's shirt (Glenn Maxwell's Victoria); Rose Bowl | Shirt sponsors, stadium names, IPL owners. |

**What this predicts for the finals**

- Expect visual connects: logos, a still from an ad or a film, a painting. Almost every Monty question had a picture slide.
- Expect literary sources inside business questions. He reads widely (Tolkien, Gaiman, Chabon, Archer) and puts it into brand questions.
- Expect at least two Thrissur or Kerala questions. Manappuram was in the prelims, so Kalyan, Joyalukkas, ESAF, South Indian Bank, CSB Bank, Dhanlaxmi, V-Guard and Wonderla are all fair game.
- Expect one or two questions on 2026's viral moments, asked through an older fact.
- Answers are usually figurable. Work out the link between the clues before you start recalling facts.

## Practice set in his style

Twenty questions written the way he writes them: shared names, name origins, an old fact behind a new headline. Do them with Devraj under a 45-second timer, then check the answers below.

1. A 2026 tech acquisition's target takes its name from an emoji first added to Unicode in 2015. Name the target.
2. Which Thrissur bank, founded in 1920, shares its largest shareholder with a British pharmacy chain founded in Nottingham in 1849?
3. Bill Rasmussen, who died in Aug 2026, started a cable channel in 1979 to show Connecticut college sport. Name it and expand its letters.
4. Hutu extremists called their victims "inyenzi". In 2026 the same insult became the name of an Indian satirical political party. What is it called?
5. A brand that turned 100 in 2026, formed by a German merger, carries the name of a car dealer's daughter. Name the brand and the girl.
6. Which fashion retailer, whose co-founder died in May 2026, takes its name from a 1960s phrase about generations?
7. A 2026 Nasdaq ticker is SPCX. Name the company and the AI firm it swallowed four months earlier.
8. Connect: Christ College's own town, a 1940 Ayurvedic soap, and an IT company that started out selling vegetable oil.
9. Which Indian state's tourism slogan was coined by Walter Mendez in 1989?
10. A Malayalam film of 2010 shows a Thrissur rice trader desperate for a Padma award. Name the film and its star.
11. Name the British online fashion retailer whose co-founder died in Thailand in Feb 2026, and expand its original name.
12. In 2026 one company came to own both CNN and CBS. What is it now called, and who runs it?
13. Which pop artist died in 2026 and was famous for polka dots on Louis Vuitton bags?
14. Which Indian company's 2026 IPO beat Hyundai Motor India's record, and whom did it overtake as India's most valuable telecom company?
15. A 1976 founding: three partners, one of whom sold his 10% for $800. Which company turned 50 in 2026, and who was the third man?
16. Whose name is used for elbow-ligament surgery, and which year did he die?
17. What does ESAF stand for, in the name of a Mannuthy-based small finance bank?
18. Which Tata Trusts chairman opposed N. Chandrasekaran's re-appointment in Sep 2026, and how is he related to Shapoor Mistry?
19. The 2026 India–EU deal cut India's car tariffs to what level? What did Modi call the deal?
20. Which Thrissur jeweller's founder spoke in Oct 2026 about Kerala as "the next business hub" because of which new port?
