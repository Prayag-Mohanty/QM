AI naming has a surprisingly small number of recurring logics: dead scientists, science-fiction coinages, mythology, and brand mascots that already existed. The Muppet thread is the best convergent chain in the subject.

## Quiz-ready stories

1. **Claude** is named after **Claude Shannon**, the founder of information theory.
2. **BERT and ERNIE are both Sesame Street references.** Google's **BERT** (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers, 2018) came first; Baidu's **ERNIE** (Enhanced Representation through kNowledge IntEgration) was named as a deliberate riposte.
3. **Grok** was coined by **Robert Heinlein** in *Stranger in a Strange Land*. It was Musk's favourite word from his favourite author as a teenager; he later received the **Heinlein Prize** himself.
4. **Gemini** is a double pun: the **twins** are the 2023 merger of **Google Brain and DeepMind**, and the name also nods to **NASA's Project Gemini**, the programme that bridged Mercury and Apollo. It was codenamed **Titan** before launch.
5. **Cortana** is three links deep: Microsoft's assistant → the AI character in **Halo** → **Cortana**, the legendary sword of the knight **Ogier the Dane**.
6. **Siri** is a real person's name. Co-founder **Dag Kittlaus** named it after a Norwegian colleague; in Norwegian it means roughly "beautiful woman who leads you to victory". He had planned it as a daughter's name and had a son instead.
7. **Watson** is named after **Thomas J. Watson Sr.**, IBM's founder and first CEO — not after Sherlock Holmes' Watson.
8. **Amazon's Rufus** was named after a **Welsh Corgi** belonging to early employees, a fixture at Amazon's first Seattle office. The chatbot was **retired on 13 May 2026**, folded into "Alexa for Shopping".
9. **Capital One's Eno** is "**One**" spelled backwards, and was deliberately built **gender-neutral** after criticism that nearly every banking chatbot had been given a female name and persona.
10. **Air India's Maharaja** chatbot reuses the airline's **1946 mascot** by Umesh Rao — the first airline to turn its own mascot into a generative-AI agent.
11. **Qwen's** Chinese name, **Tongyi Qianwen** (通义千问), means roughly "**seeking truth by asking a thousand questions**". Alibaba retired the longer branding for plain "Qwen" in **March 2026**.
12. **BHIM**, India's UPI app, is named after **B.R. Ambedkar** — not the Mahabharata's Bheem. Ambedkar's LSE thesis on the rupee problem fed into the creation of the **RBI**.

## UPI apps

| App | Origin |
| --- | --- |
| **BHIM** | Named after **B.R. Ambedkar**, not the Mahabharata's Bheem. Modi's launch speech leaned on Ambedkar's economics: his LSE thesis on the rupee problem fed into the **RBI's** creation. |
| **Google Pay** | In India it launched as **Tez** — Hindi for "**fast**" — before the 2018 global unification. The lineage runs Google Wallet (2011) → Android Pay (2015) → Tez (2017) → Google Pay. |
| **Paytm** | "**Pay Through Mobile**", founded 2010 by Vijay Shekhar Sharma, originally just mobile recharges. |
| **PhonePe** | Founded by **Sameer Nigam** and **Rahul Chari** after watching Flipkart's payment systems fail during a 2014 sale. Flipkart bought it a year later for under $20 million; Walmart inherited it in 2018. |
