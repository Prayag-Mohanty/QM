## Quiz-ready: likely questions

Name origins first, the way Major Nair likes them.

1. **Which brand's name was rearranged from the letters of "Zorba" because a nearby bar already had that name?** Zara.
2. **Which brand got its Q from a clerk's misreading of the C in its abbreviated name?** Uniqlo (Unique Clothing Warehouse).
3. **Which brand's name joins the Swedish word for "hers" with a hunting-store owner's first name?** H&M (Hennes & Mauritz).
4. **Which two brands came from a brothers' feud in Herzogenaurach?** Adidas (Adi Dassler) and Puma (Rudolf Dassler, originally "Ruda").
5. **Who designed the Swoosh, and for how much?** Carolyn Davidson, for $35.
6. **Which brand's logo was created in 1896 to stop counterfeiters?** The Louis Vuitton monogram.
7. **What did Guccio Gucci do in London that inspired his brand?** He was a lift boy and porter at the Savoy Hotel.
8. **Which luxury house's logo is a carriage with no driver, and why?** Hermès. The house supplies the carriage, and the customer drives.
9. **What is the No. 5 in Chanel No. 5?** The fifth sample Ernest Beaux presented, and Coco's lucky number.
10. **Which dress led to Google Images?** Jennifer Lopez's green Versace jungle dress at the 2000 Grammys.
11. **Where does the name Moncler come from?** The village of Monestier-de-Clermont, near Grenoble.
12. **Which brand was named Diesel because the word is the same in every language?** Diesel, Renzo Rosso's brand.
13. **What is Pantone 1837?** Tiffany Blue, named after Tiffany's founding year.
14. **Which Bottega Veneta tagline replaces a logo?** "When your own initials are enough." The woven intrecciato leather is the logo.
15. **Which date is called the birthday of blue jeans?** 20 May 1873, when Levi Strauss and Jacob Davis got the rivet patent.
16. **Whose daughter married Neville Wadia, making Nusli Wadia his grandson?** Muhammad Ali Jinnah.
17. **Which Thrissur family founded both a silk chain and a jewellery giant?** The Kalyan Group (Kalyan Silks and Kalyan Jewellers).
18. **Which Kerala garment firm launched a political party called Twenty20?** Kitex (Sabu M. Jacob).
19. **Which Kolhapur-born ex-Unilever executive runs Chanel?** Leena Nair.
20. **What does the Kering name come from, and what is its logo?** Breton "ker" (home), pronounced like "caring". The logo is an owl.

| **Givenchy** (1952) | Hubert de Givenchy. | Muse **Audrey Hepburn**: the little black dress in *Breakfast at Tiffany's*. Logo is four Gs in a square. | Owned by LVMH. CD Sarah Burton (ex-McQueen, who designed Kate Middleton's wedding dress).\* |
| **Balenciaga** (1917) | Cristóbal Balenciaga, a **Basque** from Getaria, Spain. He moved to Paris in 1937 during the Civil War. | Balloon skirt, sack dress. | **Celine** (1945) | Céline Vipiana began with **children's shoes**. | The Triomphe logo is based on the chain around the **Arc de Triomphe**. Hedi Slimane dropped the accent in 2018. | Owned by LVMH. CD Michael Rider.\* |
| **Cartier** (1847) | Louis-François Cartier. The New York Fifth Avenue shop was bought in 1917 for **$100 plus a double-strand pearl necklace**. Made the **Patiala Necklace** for Maharaja Bhupinder Singh (1928). | Owned by Richemont.\* |

The L.12.12 polo: L for Lacoste, 1 for pique, 2 for short sleeves, 12 for the 12th prototype. | Owned by Swiss family firm Maus Frères.\* |

## Italian houses

| House | Origin and name | Logo and signatures | Who runs it now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Gucci** (1921, Florence) | Guccio Gucci had worked as a **lift boy and porter at the Savoy Hotel, London**, and was inspired by the luggage of wealthy guests. | The **GG** logo (Guccio's initials) was designed in the 1960s by Aldo. The green-red-green **web stripe** comes from the saddle girth. **Bamboo bag** (1947) used bamboo because of a postwar leather shortage. Horsebit loafer. | **Prada** (1913, Milan) | Mario Prada and his brother sold leather goods in the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II. Mario barred women from the business, yet it passed to his daughter Luisa. **Miuccia Prada** (granddaughter, a PhD in political science and former mime) introduced the **nylon backpack** (1984) made from Pocone military-tent nylon. | The **triangle logo** and the old Savoy coat of arms (it was a royal supplier). **Miu Miu** is Miuccia's childhood nickname. *The Devil Wears Prada* (book 2003, film 2006, sequel 2026). Sponsors the America's Cup (Luna Rossa). | Prada Group (Hong Kong-listed). Miuccia and **Raf Simons** are co-CDs. **Bought Versace** from Capri for about €1.25 bn (closed Dec 2025).\* |
| **Versace** (1978, Milan) | **Gianni Versace** from Reggio Calabria. He was murdered in 1997 outside his Miami Beach mansion, Casa Casuarina, by Andrew Cunanan. | The **Medusa** head logo was taken from a mosaic in the ruins near his childhood home. The idea is that anyone who looks at Medusa can't turn away. The Greek key border. The **Jennifer Lopez green jungle dress** (Grammys 2000) is why **Google Images** was invented. | Prada Group. **Pieter Mulier** (ex-Alaïa) is chief creative officer from 1 Jul 2026, after Dario Vitale's single season. Donatella stepped back in 2025. |
| **Giorgio Armani** (1975) | Armani studied medicine, then worked as a window dresser at La Rinascente. | Eagle logo, said to be doodled on a phone call. Unstructured jacket. *American Gigolo* (1980) made his name. Armani hotels at the Burj Khalifa and in Milan. Owns **Olimpia Milano** basketball club. "Re Giorgio". | Armani died in **Sept 2025** at 91. His will tells the foundation to sell 15% to a buyer such as LVMH, L'Oréal or EssilorLuxottica, with a sale or IPO in 3–5 years.\* |
| **Valentino** (1960, Rome) | Valentino Garavani. | **Valentino red** (rosso Valentino), inspired by a Barcelona opera. Rockstud shoes. | Owned by Mayhoola (Qatar) with **Kering holding 30%**. CD **Alessandro Michele** (ex-Gucci). Valentino himself died in Rome on 19 Jan 2026, aged 93.\* |
| **Fendi** (1925, Rome) | Edoardo and Adele Fendi. Their **five daughters** ran it later. | The **double-F "Zucca"** logo ("fun fur") by Karl Lagerfeld, who designed for Fendi for 54 years. **Baguette** bag (1997), carried under the arm like a loaf, made famous by *Sex and the City*. | Owned by LVMH. CCO Maria Grazia Chiuri, ex-Dior, from Oct 2025. |
| **Bottega Veneta** (1966, Vicenza) | "Venetian workshop". | No logo: the **intrecciato** woven leather is the logo. Tagline "**When your own initials are enough**". Its Pouch and Jodie bags. | Owned by Kering. CD **Louise Trotter** (2025). CEO Bartolomeo Rongone left for Moncler in Mar 2026. |
| **Dolce & Gabbana** (1985) | Domenico Dolce (Sicilian) and Stefano Gabbana (Milanese). | Sicilian imagery. The 2018 **chopsticks ad** scandal in China. | Independent. |
| **Salvatore Ferragamo** (1927, Florence) | The "**shoemaker to the stars**". Went to Hollywood in 1914 and studied anatomy to make comfortable shoes. | Gancini hook. **Rainbow platform sandal** for Judy Garland (1938). Palazzo Spini Feroni museum. | Listed; family-controlled.\* |
| **Ermenegildo Zegna** (1910) | Fabric mill in **Trivero**. | The Oasi Zegna nature reserve. NYSE-listed (2021 SPAC). Owns **Tom Ford** fashion. | CEO Gildo Zegna.\* |
| **Moncler** (1952) | Named from **Mon**estier-de-**Cler**mont, a village near Grenoble. Started with sleeping bags and tents, and dressed the 1954 Italian **K2** expedition. | Rooster logo. Owns **Stone Island**. | CEO Remo Ruffini. Bartolomeo Rongone joined as incoming CEO in 2026. |
| **Benetton** (1965, Treviso) | Siblings Luciano, Giuliana, Gilberto and Carlo. Giuliana knitted the sweaters. | **United Colors of Benetton**. Oliviero Toscani's shock ads (the AIDS patient, the priest kissing a nun). The family also owned **Atlantia**, whose Morandi bridge in Genoa collapsed in 2018. | Edizione holding.\* |
| **Diesel** (1978) | Renzo Rosso. He named it Diesel because it was an alternative fuel, **pronounced the same in every language**. | "For Successful Living". | OTB Group (Only The Brave), which also owns Maison Margiela, Marni and Jil Sander. CD Glenn Martens. |

## British and American

| **Tommy Hilfiger** (1985) | Launched with a famous 1985 **Times Square billboard** that compared him with Ralph Lauren, Perry Ellis and Calvin Klein. | Red, white and blue flag logo, inspired by nautical flags. | Owned by **PVH**, as is Calvin Klein. In India, PVH works with **Arvind** for both brands. |
| **Calvin Klein** (1968) | Calvin Klein. | Brooke Shields' 1980 ad: "Nothing comes between me and my Calvins." Mark Wahlberg's underwear ads. **CK One** (1994), the first big unisex fragrance. | PVH. |

The red **Tab** (1936). The back-pocket **Arcuate** stitching is the oldest US apparel trademark. Levi's Stadium is the 49ers' home. | CEO **Michelle Gass**. Arvind was its Indian licensee in the 1990s; it is now direct.\* |
| **Gap** (1969, San Francisco) | Don and Doris Fisher named it after the "**generation gap**". In India, Gap is run by **Reliance Retail**. | CEO **Richard Dickson** (ex-Mattel, who led the Barbie revival).\* |
| **Coach** (1941, New York) | Started as a Manhattan loft workshop. It used the softness of **baseball gloves** as inspiration for leather. | Horse-and-carriage logo. | Owned by **Tapestry**, which renamed itself from Coach Inc. in 2017 and also owns Kate Spade. | **Alexander McQueen** (1992) | Lee McQueen, a Savile Row tailor. | Skull scarf. Designed Kate Middleton's wedding dress (Sarah Burton). "Bumster" trousers. | Owned by Kering.\* |
| **Vivienne Westwood**, **Paul Smith**, **Mulberry** | Westwood dressed punk (the Sex Pistols' look, with Malcolm McLaren). Mulberry's tree logo comes from Somerset. Paul Smith is known for multicoloured stripes. |  |  |
