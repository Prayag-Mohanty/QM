Eighth in the Christ Com prep set: what released in the last two months, and the brand, business or tech connect behind each one.

## Quiz-ready

If you only have ten minutes, revise these. Each one has a brand, business or tech link built in.

1. ***The Social Reckoning*** (9 Oct): Sorkin's Facebook Files film. Jeremy Strong plays Zuckerberg, Mikey Madison plays Frances Haugen, Jeremy Allen White plays *WSJ*'s Jeff Horwitz. It flopped.
2. **Capcom's double**: *Resident Evil* (18 Sep, franchise-best $60 m opening; called *Biohazard* in Japan) and *Street Fighter* (16 Oct, No. 1).
3. ***Digger***: Tom Cruise as oil tycoon Digger Rockwell, directed by Iñárritu, with a weak opening on a $125 m budget.
4. ***Coyote vs. Acme***: shelved by Warner Bros for a tax write-off, rescued by Ketchup Entertainment. Wile E. Coyote sues Acme.
5. ***Wildwood***: from **Laika**, the stop-motion studio owned by Nike's Phil Knight and run by his son Travis.
6. ***Clayface***: DC Studios, released by a Warner Bros that is now part of **Skydance**.
7. ***Wonka's The Golden Ticket***: a Netflix reality show for the 1971 film's 55th anniversary, with a recreated Gene Wilder voice. Quaker Oats funded the original. Netflix owns the Roald Dahl Story Company.
8. ***Cupertino***: CBS legal drama about suing Big Tech, named after Apple's home town.
9. ***Get Jiro***: Anthony Bourdain's world where chefs rule Los Angeles.
10. ***Spider-Man: Brand New Day***: the biggest US superhero gross ever. Sony owns the rights; Marvel co-produces.
11. ***The One About Matthew Perry***: a Netflix documentary titled in *Friends* episode style.
12. ***Animals***: Ben Affleck's Netflix thriller, made by **Artists Equity**.
13. ***Drishyam 3: The Conclusion*** (Hindi, 2 Oct): about ₹379 crore net. *Drishyam* was the first Indian film remade in Chinese (*Sheep Without a Shepherd*).
14. ***Jailer 2***: Sun Pictures (owners of Sunrisers Hyderabad), ₹666 crore in 10 days, and the biggest Tamil film ever in Kerala. *Jailer* 1's bonuses were a BMW X7 and a Porsche.
15. ***Mirzapur The Movie***: a Prime Video series turned into a cinema release.
16. ***Thudakkam***: Vismaya Mohanlal's debut. Its producer, Antony Perumbavoor, was Mohanlal's driver.
17. ***Toxic***: Yash's film, directed by Malayali Geetu Mohandas.
18. ***East of Eden***: Zoe Kazan adapts the book her grandfather Elia Kazan filmed.

## Hollywood and global films

The autumn's business stories: **Capcom** had two video-game adaptations in four weeks (*Resident Evil*, *Street Fighter*). Aaron Sorkin's Facebook sequel **flopped**. And a film Warner Bros shelved for a tax write-off, *Coyote vs. Acme*, finally reached cinemas.

| Film (US release) | Studio | Box office | Brand / business / tech connect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ***Resident Evil*** (18 Sep) | Sony / Constantin | $60 m US opening, the franchise's best. About $157 m US by late Oct. | Based on **Capcom**'s game, called *Biohazard* in Japan. Director **Zach Cregger** (*Barbarian*, *Weapons*). Its global gross passed every earlier *Resident Evil* film within a week. Budget about $75 m. |
| ***Practical Magic 2*** (Sep) | Warner Bros | No. 1 on 13 Sep weekend ($30 m) | Sandra Bullock and Nicole Kidman return 28 years after the 1998 original. |
| ***Verity*** (2 Oct) | Amazon MGM | $32 m opening; about $74 m US | A **Colleen Hoover** novel, a BookTok bestseller. Starring Anne Hathaway. |
| ***Digger*** (2 Oct) | Warner Bros / Legendary | Only about $8 m opening on a reported **$125 m** budget | Tom Cruise plays **Digger Rockwell**, an **oil tycoon** trying to prove he is humanity's saviour after a disaster he caused. Directed by Alejandro G. Iñárritu, a satire of billionaire saviours. |
| ***The Social Reckoning*** (9 Oct) | Sony / Columbia | Weak: about $8 m in its first 10 days | **Aaron Sorkin**'s follow-up to *The Social Network*, about the 2021 **"Facebook Files"** leak. **Mikey Madison** plays whistleblower **Frances Haugen**, **Jeremy Allen White** plays *WSJ* reporter **Jeff Horwitz**, and **Jeremy Strong** plays **Mark Zuckerberg** (Jesse Eisenberg didn't return). Bill Burr plays Facebook's lawyer. |
| ***Street Fighter*** (16 Oct) | Paramount / Legendary | No. 1: $31 m US opening, $50 m worldwide; budget about $82 m | **Capcom**'s second film in a month. Andrew Koji is Ryu, Noah Centineo is Ken, Callina Liang is Chun-Li; directed by Kitao Sakurai. The 1994 and 2009 versions flopped. Capcom's name comes from "Capsule Computers". |
| ***Clayface*** (23 Oct) | DC Studios / Warner Bros | No. 1 with about $43 m worldwide opening | The third film in James Gunn and Peter Safran's DC Universe: a body-horror villain film. Warner Bros is now part of **Skydance** after the Paramount merger closed on 6 Oct. |
| ***Wildwood*** (23 Oct) | **Laika** | About $27 m US in 10 days | A stop-motion studio owned by **Nike co-founder Phil Knight**. His son **Travis Knight** runs it. Based on a novel by Colin Meloy of the band The Decemberists. |
| ***Coyote vs. Acme*** (late Aug) | **Ketchup Entertainment** | About $60 m US | Warner Bros finished it, then **shelved it in 2023 for a tax write-off**. After an outcry it was sold to Ketchup. The plot: Wile E. Coyote sues the **Acme Corporation** over its faulty products. John Cena and Will Forte star. |
| ***Spider-Man: Brand New Day*** (31 Jul) | Sony with Marvel Studios | About $955 m US by October. Its $360 m opening was the biggest of 2026, with record single-day takings. | It became the **highest-grossing superhero film ever in the US**, and was No. 2 in India in 2026. Spider-Man's film rights belong to **Sony**, shared with Disney's Marvel through a co-production deal. |
| ***The Odyssey*** (17 Jul) | Universal | About $620 m US | Christopher Nolan, the first feature shot entirely on **IMAX film cameras**. |
| ***Heart of the Beast***, ***Forgotten Island***, ***Other Mommy***, ***Sense and Sensibility*** | Various | Mid-sized hits | *Forgotten Island* is a DreamWorks animation (owned by Universal/Comcast). |
| **Re-releases and events** | — | *Avengers: Endgame* "Encore" re-release; BTS *Arirang* concert screenings | Event cinema helps theatre chains. BTS's label is **HYBE**. |

**Box-office context:** the 2026 US summer was the biggest ever, led by *Spider-Man* and *The Odyssey*.

## Series and streaming

The two most "business" shows are both named after places and products: CBS's *Cupertino*, about lawyers suing Silicon Valley, and Netflix's ***Wonka's The Golden Ticket***, a reality show that recreated Gene Wilder's voice for the 1971 film's 55th anniversary.

| ***Wonka's The Golden Ticket*** (23 Sep) | Netflix | A reality competition: 12 Golden Ticket winners and partners compete inside a chocolate factory. Filmed on the Gold Coast, Australia. | Marks the **55th anniversary** of *Willy Wonka & the Chocolate Factory* (1971), which **Quaker Oats** funded to launch the Wonka Bar. It uses a **recreated Gene Wilder voice**, with his estate's consent. Netflix bought the **Roald Dahl Story Company** in 2021. |

| ***The One About Matthew Perry*** (Oct) | Netflix | A three-part documentary on the *Friends* star. No. 1 English show in late October. | The title copies the format of every *Friends* episode title ("The One With…"). Perry's memoir: *Friends, Lovers, and the Big Terrible Thing* (2022). |

| ***Below*** (8 Oct) | Netflix | A sea-creature thriller set in Newfoundland, with Josh Hartnett. | No. 1 English show in early October (17 m views). |

## Indian films and series

The two big Indian films of the autumn are both sequels: Ajay Devgn's ***Drishyam 3: The Conclusion*** (2 Oct) and Rajinikanth's ***Jailer 2*** (Dussehra). The biggest business story is ***Mirzapur The Movie*** (4 Sep), a streaming series turned into a cinema release.

| Title (release) | Producer / platform | Box office | Business / trivia hook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ***Drishyam 3: The Conclusion*** (Hindi, 2 Oct) | Panorama Studios | About ₹379 crore India net; about ₹550 crore worldwide | Ajay Devgn's first film to cross ₹300 crore net. It beat *Border 2* and *Hanuman Ansh*. The **Malayalam** *Drishyam 3* (Mohanlal, Jeethu Joseph) came out first, in May 2026, at about ₹230 crore worldwide. *Drishyam* (2013) was the first Indian film remade in Chinese, as ***Sheep Without a Shepherd*** (2019). |
| ***Jailer 2*** (Tamil, Dussehra, Oct) | **Sun Pictures** (Kalanithi Maran) | ₹666 crore worldwide in 10 days, 2026's #2 Indian film | Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar. It is the highest-grossing Tamil film ever in **Kerala** (₹63 crore in 10 days, beating *Leo*), and the second-biggest Tamil film worldwide after *2.0*. Sun Pictures also owns the IPL's **Sunrisers Hyderabad**. After *Jailer* (2023), Maran gave Rajinikanth a **BMW X7** and Nelson a **Porsche**. |
| ***Mirzapur The Movie*** (4 Sep) | Excel Entertainment (Farhan Akhtar and Ritesh Sidhwani) | About ₹231 crore India net | The **Amazon Prime Video** series made the jump to cinemas, a first for a major Indian streaming franchise. |
| ***Toxic*** (Kannada/English; Hindi dub 26 Aug) | KVN Productions | About ₹250 crore India net | Yash's film after *KGF*, directed by **Geetu Mohandas**, a Malayali director. It was shot in English and Kannada at once. |
| ***Hanuman Ansh*** (7 Aug) | — | About ₹343 crore India net | One of 2026's biggest Hindi hits. |
| ***Thudakkam*** (Malayalam, 7 Aug, Onam) | **Aashirvad Cinemas** (Antony Perumbavoor) | About ₹41 crore | The acting debut of **Vismaya Mohanlal**, Mohanlal's daughter; directed by Jude Anthany Joseph. Mohanlal has a cameo as her martial-arts teacher. Antony Perumbavoor began as **Mohanlal's driver** before becoming his producer. |
| ***Dhurandhar: The Revenge*** (Mar 2026) | Jio Studios and B62 (Aditya Dhar) | ₹1,376 crore India gross, India's biggest film of 2026 | The year's benchmark: every autumn release is compared with it. |
| **Malayalam on streaming (Oct)** | Netflix | — | *Avarachan & Sons*, *Dhoomakethu*, *Baththa* and *Pradhama Drishtiya Kuttakkar* all reached Netflix in October. |
| ***The Great Indian Kapil Show***, ***Roadies: Rebirth***, ***Hustle 5*** | Netflix / JioHotstar / MTV | — | The big Indian reality and talk formats running this autumn. |
