## Quiz-ready: likely GI questions

1. **What was India's first GI?** Darjeeling Tea (2004–05).
2. **Where is India's GI Registry?** Chennai.
3. **Which Act governs GIs in India, and when did it come into force?** The GI of Goods Act 1999, in force from 15 Sep 2003.
4. **How many GIs did India have in the May 2026 register?** 822. Uttar Pradesh leads with 79.
5. **Which state got the most new GIs in 2025–26?** Madhya Pradesh (26), out of a record 125.
6. **Which Thrissur snap melon, used for juice, got a GI in 2022–23?** Kodungallur Pottuvellari.
7. **Which Thrissur nendran banana is offered at Guruvayur?** Chengalikodan.
8. **Which Kerala GI is a mirror made of metal?** Aranmula Kannadi.
9. **Which Kerala rice is used in Ayurvedic massage?** Navara.
10. **Which Kerala rice is grown in saline fields alternating with prawns?** Pokkali (and Kaipad in Kannur).
11. **Which Malappuram GI comes from the world's oldest teak plantation?** Nilambur Teak (Conolly's Plot).
12. **Which Kerala drum, the backbone of Kathakali music, is a GI?** Palakkad Maddalam.
13. **Which two states fought over the rasgulla?** West Bengal (2017) and Odisha (2019).
14. **Which US company's Basmati patent pushed India towards a GI law?** RiceTec.
15. **Which Indian GI chappal did Prada show on a Milan runway in 2025?** Kolhapuri chappal.
16. **Which was the first Indian alcoholic drink with a GI?** Goa's feni.
17. **Which cheese started France's AOC system?** Roquefort (1925).
18. **Which country lost the feta fight at the EU court?** Denmark.
19. **Why can't cheese from Stilton village be called Stilton?** The village is outside the designated counties.
20. **Which perfume did YSL have to rename after a Champagne lawsuit?** "Champagne" (1993), later sold as Yvresse.
21. **Which Malabar spice brought Vasco da Gama to Kerala?** Malabar pepper ("black gold").
22. **Which Kerala GI weave is linked to the 2018-flood "Chekutty" dolls?** Chendamangalam handloom.

## GI basics

- **What it is**: a sign that a product's **quality or reputation comes from its place of origin**. It's a type of IP protected under the **WTO TRIPS** agreement (Articles 22–24). Wine and spirits get stronger protection under Article 23.
- **Indian law**: the **Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999**, in force from **15 Sep 2003**. India passed it partly after the **Basmati patent fight with RiceTec** (US, 1997).
- **GI Registry**: **Chennai**, under the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks (DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce).
- **Term**: **10 years**, renewable indefinitely.
- **India's first GI**: **Darjeeling Tea** (2004–05).
- **Logo**: India's GI logo carries the tagline **"Invaluable Treasures of Incredible India"** (2018).\*
- **Total**: **822** registered GIs as of the official register of **2 May 2026**.
- **2025–26**: a record **125** new GIs. **Madhya Pradesh** led with 26, then West Bengal (24) and Himachal (13).
- **State leaders overall (May 2026)**: **Uttar Pradesh 79**, Tamil Nadu 74, West Bengal 58, Maharashtra 52, Madhya Pradesh 50.
- **Kerala** topped the country in **2022–23** with 5 new tags, out of only 12 nationwide.
- **Foreign GIs registered in India** include **Champagne, Cognac, Scotch Whisky, Napa Valley wines, Prosecco, Parmigiano Reggiano, Peruvian Pisco, Brandy de Jerez and Provolone Valpadana**.\*
- **Recent new tags**:
  - **2026**: Sailana Balam Kakadi (MP), Bishnupur Dashavatar Tash (Bengal playing cards), Sirsa Kinnow (Haryana).
  - **2025–26**: Khajuraho Stone Craft, Santiniketan Batik, Banglar Nolen Gur, Balagarh Boat and Jharkhand Dokra.

*\* From general knowledge; the rest is from the sources below.*

## India's famous GIs and GI fights

### The fights (quizmasters love these)

| Dispute | Story |
| --- | --- |
| **Basmati** | In **1997** the US firm **RiceTec** won a patent on "Basmati rice lines and grains". India challenged it, and most claims were withdrawn (2001). It spurred India's GI Act. India filed for an **EU PGI** (2018); **Pakistan opposed** it and filed its own. India registered Basmati as a GI covering Punjab, Haryana, Himachal, Delhi, Uttarakhand, western UP and J&K.\* |
| **Rasgulla** | **West Bengal** got **Banglar Rasogolla** (2017). **Odisha** got **Odisha Rasagola** (2019), backed by its claim to the **Jagannath temple's Niladri Bije** ritual offering. |
| **Darjeeling Tea** | India's **first GI**. Protected in the EU as a PGI (2011). Only tea from **87 gardens** (now fewer) in the Darjeeling hills can use the name. The "**Darjeeling lady**" logo.\* |
| **Tirupati Laddu** | GI-tagged in **2009**. The 2024 controversy over ghee quality.\* |
| **Kolhapuri chappal** | GI 2019 (Maharashtra and Karnataka). In **2025** Prada showed similar sandals at Milan without credit. After the backlash, it acknowledged the Indian inspiration and talked to artisans.\* |
| **Mysore Pak, Mysore silk, Mysore sandal soap** | **Mysore Sandal Soap** (KSDL, 1916) is a rare GI for a **branded factory product**.\* |
| **Feni** | Goa's **cashew feni** was the **first alcoholic drink from India** to get a GI (2009).\* |
| **Banarasi saree vs Kanchipuram** | Both are GIs. Kanchipuram silk got its tag in 2005. Banarasi's was disputed by weavers in Mubarakpur, Azamgarh, who are included.\* |

## World GIs and famous disputes

**EU terms**: **PDO** (Protected Designation of Origin) means everything is produced in the region. **PGI** (Protected Geographical Indication) means at least one stage is. France's **AOC** (Appellation d'Origine Contrôlée) system began with **Roquefort cheese in 1925**, though its 1411 royal charter is often called the first GI.\*

| GI | Origin | The story / dispute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Champagne** | France | Only sparkling wine from Champagne made by the **méthode champenoise**. The French body **CIVC** sues relentlessly: it forced **Yves Saint Laurent** to drop a perfume called "Champagne" (1993). A Swiss village called **Champagne** isn't allowed to label its wine as such. In Russia, a 2021 law made Moët relabel itself "sparkling wine".\* |
| **Cognac** | France | Brandy from the Charente. Registered as a GI in India.\* |
| **Scotch Whisky** | Scotland | Must be matured **3+ years in oak in Scotland**. The Scotch Whisky Association polices the term worldwide. India registered it as a GI (2010).\* |
| **Parmigiano Reggiano** | Italy | The "Parmesan" name fights in the US and Germany. Wheels can carry **microchip** labels against counterfeits.\* |
| **Roquefort** | France | **Sheep's milk** cheese aged in the **Combalou caves**. The first AOC (1925).\* |
| **Feta** | Greece | The EU gave Greece exclusive use in **2002 and 2005**. **Denmark** was taken to court for exporting "feta" (CJEU ruled in 2022).\* |
| **Prosecco** | Italy | Fighting **Croatia's Prosek** (a sweet wine) since 2021.\* |
| **Tequila / Mezcal** | Mexico | Only from **blue agave** in Jalisco and specific states. Mexico's first appellation (1974).\* |
| **Pisco** | Peru vs Chile | Both claim it. India recognised **Peruvian Pisco**.\* |
| **Darjeeling** | India | An EU PGI since 2011. |
| **Kobe beef** | Japan | Tajima-strain Wagyu from Hyōgo. "Kobe" burgers sold abroad are often fake.\* |
| **Stilton / Cornish pasty / Melton Mowbray pork pie** | UK | Ironically, cheese made in the village of **Stilton** can't be called Stilton, because it's in the wrong county.\* |
| **Port / Madeira / Sherry (Jerez)** | Portugal / Spain | **Brandy de Jerez** was registered in India (2022–23).\* |
| **Bordeaux, Napa Valley, Rioja** |  | Napa Valley is registered as a GI in India.\* |
| **Swiss** watches | Switzerland | "Swiss made" requires at least **60%** of the value to come from Switzerland (the 2017 "Swissness" law).\* |
| **Basmati** | India and Pakistan | Pending in the EU (see above). |
| **Ceylon tea**, **Colombian coffee** (Juan Valdez), **Jamaica Blue Mountain coffee**, **Idaho potatoes**, **Florida oranges** |  | Coffee and tea origin marks used as GIs or certification marks.\* |
