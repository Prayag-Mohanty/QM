## The biggest banks, US and Europe

The four largest banks in the world are all Chinese and state-owned. **ICBC** is #1 at about $7.65 trillion in assets (S&P Global, year-end 2025). JPMorgan is the largest outside China. SBI and HDFC Bank are India's only entries in the global top 100.

## Central banks and development banks

The big 2026 change is at the US Federal Reserve: **Kevin Warsh** replaced Jerome Powell as chair. The classic quiz facts are the oldest central bank (Sweden's **Riksbank**, 1668) and the Bank of England's nickname, "**The Old Lady of Threadneedle Street**".

### Central banks

| Central bank | Founded | Head (Nov 2026) | Trivia |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sveriges Riksbank** (Sweden) | 1668 | — | The **oldest central bank**. It funds the **Nobel economics prize**, officially the "Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel" (from 1969). |
| **Bank of England** | 1694 | Andrew Bailey | "**The Old Lady of Threadneedle Street**", after a 1797 cartoon. Founded to lend money to William III for war. |
| **US Federal Reserve** | 1913 | **Kevin Warsh**, chair from 2026 (succeeding Jerome Powell) | The idea came from a secret 1910 meeting of bankers on **Jekyll Island**. Twelve regional Fed banks. |
| **European Central Bank** | 1998, Frankfurt | **Christine Lagarde** (term to 2027) | Sets rates for the 21 eurozone countries, including Bulgaria since Jan 2026. |
| **Bank of Japan** | 1882 | Kazuo Ueda | Pioneered quantitative easing (2001) and negative rates. |
| **People's Bank of China** | 1948 | Pan Gongsheng | Runs the digital yuan (e-CNY). |
| **Swiss National Bank** | 1907 | Martin Schlegel | Famous for printing money to stop the franc rising. |
| **Reserve Bank of India** | 1 April 1935, Kolkata (moved to Mumbai in 1937) | **Sanjay Malhotra** (governor since Dec 2024) | Based on the Hilton Young Commission, with ideas from **B.R. Ambedkar**'s *The Problem of the Rupee*. Nationalised in 1949. Its logo: a **tiger** and a **palm tree**. See the Indian banks doc. |
| **Bank for International Settlements (BIS)** | 1930, **Basel** | — | "The central bank of central banks". Set up for German war reparations. The Basel I/II/III bank-capital rules are named after its home city. |

### Collapses and scandals

| Event | Year | Hook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Barings Bank** | 1995 | Rogue trader **Nick Leeson** in Singapore lost £827 m and brought down Britain's oldest merchant bank, the Queen's bank. ING bought it for £1. Film: *Rogue Trader* (1999). |
| **BCCI** | 1991 | Bank of Credit and Commerce International, nicknamed the "Bank of Crooks and Criminals International". Founded by Pakistan's **Agha Hasan Abedi**. |
| **Banco Ambrosiano** | 1982 | Linked to the Vatican Bank. Its chairman Roberto Calvi, "God's banker", was found hanging under London's **Blackfriars Bridge**. |
| **Northern Rock** | 2007 | The first run on a British bank in about 150 years. |
| **Lehman Brothers** | 2008 | The largest US bankruptcy ever. |
| **Société Générale / UBS rogue traders** | 2008 / 2011 | Jérôme Kerviel (€4.9 bn loss) / Kweku Adoboli ($2.3 bn loss). |
| **LIBOR rigging** | 2012 | Barclays, UBS and others fixed the benchmark interest rate. LIBOR has since been replaced. |
| **Silicon Valley Bank** | March 2023 | A digital-age bank run, spread on social media. Signature Bank and First Republic followed. HSBC bought SVB's UK arm for **£1**. |
| **Credit Suisse** | March 2023 | The 167-year-old bank was taken over by UBS. Founded in 1856 by Alfred Escher to fund Swiss railways. |
| **Wells Fargo fake accounts** | 2016 | Staff opened millions of fake accounts to hit targets. |
| **1MDB** | 2015– | Malaysia's fund scandal involving Jho Low and Goldman Sachs. Book: *Billion Dollar Whale*. |
